About DigitalDesk

DigitalDesk is your personal productivity hub, offering a secure and efficient way to organize your digital life.

Key Features:

  • Secure Local Storage: All your data stays on your device
  • Cross-Platform: Works in any modern web browser
  • Intuitive Interface: Easy to use with powerful features
  • Backup & Sync: Choose your own data folder for backups

Export Data?

This downloads a full backup of your data as a JSON file. For large data, this can take a moment and use up meaningful disk space - exporting repeatedly (for example, by clicking this button several times) will pile up multiple large backup files unnecessarily.

Simple alternative: if your data is stored in a connected folder, you can just make a zip file of that folder yourself and share it - anyone can unzip it and use it directly, no export needed.
